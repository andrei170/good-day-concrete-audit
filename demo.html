What we do

Five things, done properly.

Concrete is not a list of twenty-two keywords. This is the work we take on and the work we are good at.

Driveways

New pours and full replacements. Old slab broken out and hauled away, base prepared properly, and jointed so Denver's freeze-thaw does not crack it in three years.

Patios & walkways

Plain, stamped or decorative. Poured to drain away from the house, which is the part most cracked patios got wrong on day one.

Foundations & repair

New foundations, foundation repair and concrete leveling. The work nobody sees and everything else depends on.

Excavation

Driveway and pool excavation, site prep and concrete removal. One crew for the digging and the pouring instead of two contractors blaming each other.

Garage & epoxy floors

Garage slabs and epoxy flooring for homes and commercial units. Sealed finishes that survive salt, oil and a Colorado winter.
